How do ramen ticket machines work in Japan?

At some ramen restaurants in Japan, you choose your meal and pay at a ticket machine before you sit down. The machine prints a ticket that you give to a staff member. Other shops use table ordering or staff-assisted ordering instead.

Useful Japanese words to recognize

味噌ラーメン means miso ramen; 大盛り means a large portion; 味玉 is a seasoned egg; 替え玉 means extra noodles at participating shops; and 現金のみ means cash only.

Do you need to speak Japanese to use a ramen ticket machine?

Not always. Some machines offer English menus or photos, while others have Japanese-only labels. This free practice game teaches common words, but actual menus, prices, and payment methods vary by restaurant.
